# Bank-Customer-Churn-Analysis-
Bank Customer Churn Analysis Using SQL,PYTHON &amp; POWERBI 

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **bank customer churn** to understand customer behavior and identify the key factors associated with customer attrition.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **Python, SQL Server, and Power BI**. Python was used for data cleaning and preparation, SQL was used for exploratory analysis, and Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard and generate business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

* Analyze overall customer churn
* Identify customer segments with higher churn
* Understand churn patterns across geography, age, gender, and tenure
* Analyze the relationship between churn and number of products
* Compare churn based on active membership and credit card ownership
* Analyze customer balance and credit score
* Identify high-value customers based on estimated salary

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. The overall customer churn rate is approximately **20.37%**, indicating that customer retention is an important area for the bank.

2. **Germany** has the highest number of churned customers among the countries shown in the dashboard, followed closely by France.

3. Customers in the **31–45 age group** represent a significant portion of churned customers.

4. Customers with a **single product** account for the largest share of churn among the product categories analyzed.

5. Customers with **3–6 years of tenure** show a notable level of churn, suggesting that retention strategies should not focus only on new customers.

6. The dashboard shows a substantial difference between customers with a **balance and zero-balance customers**, providing an opportunity to investigate whether account balance is associated with churn.

7. The analysis can help the bank identify high-risk customer segments and develop targeted **customer retention strategies**.
